Have you met our co-founder, Joseph Hayes? He holds an MSc in epidemiology from the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine, and a PhD in psychiatric epidemiology from UCL. When he’s not putting these smarts to work at juli to revolutionize the way people manage their chronic diseases, he is a Wellcome Clinical Fellow at the Division of Psychiatry at UCL, sitting on the World Economic Forum Global Future Council on Mental Health, and serving on the editorial board of the British Journal of Psychiatry and the International Journal of Bipolar Disorders.
