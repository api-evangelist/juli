A new study from our Co-founder and CSO finds that replacing sedentary behaviour with light activity or moderate-to-vigorous activity can reduce symptoms of depression and anxiety. The study, published in BMC Medicine involved 60,235 adults from the UK Biobank whose daily activity was measured by an accelerometer. The research team then used recently developed statistical methods to estimate the effect on depression and anxiety if the participant switched their sedentary time for more active behaviours or sleep.
