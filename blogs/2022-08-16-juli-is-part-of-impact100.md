We are super proud to be chosen for the Impact 100 list of Norrsken VC. The Impact100 is an annual ranking of the world's most promising impact startups, designed to shine a light on the entrepreneurs working to solve our world's greatest challenges - the true role models of our time. Norrsken was founded by Niklas Adalberth, who says one of the aims of his nonprofit is to ensure money goes to startups that take the environment and social justice into account.
