A new study from our Co-founder and CSO finds that people with low aerobic and muscular fitness are nearly twice as likely to experience depression. Low fitness levels also predicted a 60% greater chance of anxiety, over a seven-year follow-up, according to the findings published in BMC Medicine . The study involved 152,978 participants aged 40 to 69 of the UK Biobank.
